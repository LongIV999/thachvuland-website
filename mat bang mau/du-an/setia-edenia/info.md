# Setia Edenia (EcoXuân)

**Developer:** S P Setia Berhad (Setia Lai Thieu Co., Ltd)
**Location:** EcoXuân Urban Area, Frontage of National Highway 13, Lai Thieu, Thuan An, Binh Duong.
**Scale:** ~12,389 m², 3 Blocks (21-28 floors), ~740-865 units.
**Status:** Upcoming / Open for Booking.

## Key Features
- **Connectivity:** 30 mins to HCMC, near Lotte Mart & AEON Mall.
- **Amenities:** Sky swimming pool, Gym & Spa, BBQ area, Playground, Multi-layer security.
- **Green Living:** Integrated with EcoXuân's green environment.
