# Setia Edenia (EcoXuân Phase 3) - Project Information

## General Information
*   **Project Name:** Setia Edenia (EcoXuân Phase 3)
*   **Location:** Frontage of National Highway 13, Binh Hoa Ward, Thuan An City, Binh Duong Province.
*   **Developer:** Setia Lái Thiêu Co., Ltd (Subsidiary of S P Setia Berhad - Malaysia).
*   **Operating Year:** 2026 (Sales ongoing).

## Scale
*   **Total Urban Area (EcoXuân):** 10.8 hectares.
*   **Setia Edenia Block:** 3 high-rise towers (27-28 floors).
*   **Number of Units:** ~865 luxury apartments & shophouses.
*   **Building Density:** Low density, focus on green space.

## Location Highlights
Strategic location on the "Billion-Dollar Corridor" Highway 13:
*   Direct access to National Highway 13.
*   5 minutes to Lotte Mart and Becamex International Hospital.
*   10 minutes to Aeon Mall Binh Duong Canary and Song Be Golf Resort.
*   Easily connected to Ho Chi Minh City via Pham Van Dong or Highway 13.

## Amenities (International Standard)
*   **Eco-living:** Sky Park system, 10.8ha green urban area.
*   **Recreation:** Resort-style swimming pool, BBQ pavilions, Zen garden.
*   **Health:** Modern gym & yoga studio with garden views.
*   **Kids:** International standard children's playground (Kid Zone).
*   **Lifestyle:** Proximity to Lotte Mart, Aeon Mall, retail shophouses.
*   **Security:** Multi-layered 24/7 security with Smart Access system.
