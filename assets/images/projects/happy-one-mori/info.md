# Happy One Mori Bình Dương - Project Information

## General Information
*   **Project Name:** Happy One Mori
*   **Developer:** Vạn Xuân Group
*   **Location:** Lái Thiêu 14 Street, Lái Thiêu Ward, Thuận An City, Bình Dương Province.
*   **Architectural Style:** Japanese "Mori" (Forest) - Modern Tropical.

## Scale
*   **Total Area:** 10,581.6 m².
*   **Structure:** 2 blocks, 33 floors high, 2 basements.
*   **Total Units:** 1,400+ products.
    *   1,144 Apartments (Studio, 1BR+, 2BR, 3BR).
    *   226 Officetels.
    *   29 Shophouses.
*   **Expected Handover:** Q2/2028 - Q4/2028.

## Location Highlights
Strategic location at the gateway of Thuận An, bordering TP.HCM:
*   Easy connection to Ho Chi Minh City via National Highway 13.
*   Close to Becamex International Hospital, Lotte Mart, and Aeon Mall Binh Duong.
*   Located in a developed residential area with full amenities in Lái Thiêu.

## Amenities (Premium Standard)
*   **Japanese Mori Style:** High greenery density, forest-themed landscapes.
*   **Pools:** Heated swimming pool (Infinity style).
*   **Parks:** Sky Park on the rooftop, Forest Garden, Wish Tree area.
*   **Health & Wellness:** Yoga studio, modern gym center, meditative walking paths.
*   **Community:** BBQ area, central playground, luxury lounge.
*   **Smart Living:** 24/7 multi-layered security and smart home integration.

## Pricing
*   **Starting Price:** From **38.6 million VND/m²** (estimated).
*   **Ownership:** Long-term for Vietnamese, 50-year for foreigners.
